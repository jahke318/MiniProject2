# markmfredrickson_ritools Reflection

RItools had a nine-month longest inactivity gap from October 2014 through June 2015 and multiple other gaps of at least three months. Before the longest gap, development involved statistical-method improvements, bug fixes, and transformation behavior. After the gap, development resumed with feature additions, testing, documentation, and fixes.

The project recovered with both returning contributors and at least one new contributor. This makes the recovery appear to be renewed collaborative development rather than a one-time maintenance commit. The project has continued receiving bug fixes and enhancements and is classified as active.
