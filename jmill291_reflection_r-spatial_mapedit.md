# r-spatial_mapedit Reflection

The project had a 12-month inactivity gap from May 2020 through April 2021 and several other gaps of at least three months. Before the gap, commits focused on bug fixes, release preparation, and maintenance. After the gap, a new contributor began adding geometry, row editing, forms, and other functionality.

The contributor change makes the recovery especially important. Rather than the same developers simply returning after a pause, a new contributor helped restart development with substantial feature work. Later maintenance continued from additional contributors, and the project is classified as active.
